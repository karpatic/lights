# Do not manufacture from these fixture files

These files describe the superseded 36 × 25 mm board with 2.54 mm pogo pitch and 1.50 mm pads. They are retained only to preserve design history and aid recovery.

The active portrait proposal is approximately 18 × 32 mm with a 2.00 mm-pitch, 1.20 × 1.20 mm bottom-pad field. Regenerate every fixture artifact from the final verified PCB source and the selected physical pogo probe before printing or machining a fixture.
